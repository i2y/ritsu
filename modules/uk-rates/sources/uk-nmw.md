# National Minimum Wage and National Living Wage rates

Copied from <https://www.gov.uk/national-minimum-wage-rates> on 21 September 2026.
Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

The page also states: "Apprentices are entitled to the apprentice rate if they're either:
aged under 19, aged 19 or over and in the first year of their apprenticeship", and
"Apprentices are entitled to the minimum wage for their age if they both: are aged 19 or
over, have completed the first year of their apprenticeship".

## From 1 April 2026

| Age group | Hourly rate |
|---|---|
| 21 and over | £12.71 |
| 18 to 20 | £10.85 |
| Under 18 | £8 |
| Apprentice | £8 |

## 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026

| Age group | Hourly rate |
|---|---|
| 21 and over | £12.21 |
| 18 to 20 | £10 |
| Under 18 | £7.55 |
| Apprentice | £7.55 |
