# Stamp Duty Land Tax: residential property rates

Copied from <https://www.gov.uk/stamp-duty-land-tax/residential-property-rates> on
21 September 2026. England and Northern Ireland; Scotland and Wales have taxes of their own.
Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

## Rates for a single property

| Property or lease premium or transfer value | SDLT rate |
|---|---|
| Up to £125,000 | Zero |
| The next £125,000 (£125,001 to £250,000) | 2% |
| The next £675,000 (£250,001 to £925,000) | 5% |
| The next £575,000 (£925,001 to £1.5 million) | 10% |
| The remaining amount (above £1.5 million) | 12% |

## Rates for first-time buyers

| Property price band | SDLT rate |
|---|---|
| Up to £300,000 | 0% |
| £300,001 to £500,000 | 5% |

The page states: "If the price is over £500,000, you cannot claim" the relief, and "You'll
usually have to pay 5% on top of SDLT rates if buying a new residential property means you'll
own more than one".
