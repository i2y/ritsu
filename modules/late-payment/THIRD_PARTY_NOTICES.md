# Third-party notices

The module carries copies of the Act and of GOV.UK's guide, in Markdown, with the table the rule cites
taken out into `.fragments`. The rule reads the copies, and pins each by its SHA-256:

- [rules/sources/late-payment-act-1998.md](rules/sources/late-payment-act-1998.md): sections 4 and 5A of the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998, in the subsections the rules read (<https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/20/section/4>, <https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1998/20/section/5A>)
- [rules/sources/late-commercial-payments.md](rules/sources/late-commercial-payments.md): GOV.UK's guide (<https://www.gov.uk/late-commercial-payments-interest-debt-recovery/print>)

Each was copied on 18 October 2026. legislation.gov.uk and GOV.UK say that their content is available
under the Open Government Licence v3.0, except where otherwise stated
(<https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/>). The licence asks that
the source be acknowledged with the attribution statement the provider gives, or else with this one,
and, where possible, a link to the licence:

```
Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
```
