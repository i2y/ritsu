Policies

Each policy as the .gate writes it, beside the Cedar sekisho gen --target cedar generates for it. A permit allows the combinations it applies to; a forbid denies those it applies to, and wins where a permit applies too. Cedar denies a combination no policy applies to. A condition on a rule's answer names the value as the .rule writes it (within_limit) in the .gate, and as the string Cedar is given ("within_limit") in the Cedar.

permit staff_view_orders

The staff look at any order

  • On view_order, it allows 7 combinations, and no other permit allows any of them.

permit customers_view_their_orders

A customer looks at their own orders

  • On view_order, it allows 1 combination, which no other permit allows.

permit clerks_refund_within_their_limit

A clerk refunds up to the clerk's own limit, while the refund period lasts

  • On refund_order, it allows 36 combinations; no other permit allows 12 of them.

permit managers_refund_in_period

A manager refunds any amount while the refund period lasts

  • On refund_order, it allows 48 combinations; no other permit allows 24 of them.

permit managers_refund_late_on_business_days

After the refund period, a manager may still refund, on a business day in England and Wales

  • On refund_order, it allows 24 combinations, and no other permit allows any of them.

permit returns_refunds_returned_orders

The returns workflow refunds a returned order, up to 50 pounds

  • On refund_order, it allows 4 combinations, and no other permit allows any of them.

permit auditors_export_refunds

An auditor exports the record of refunds

  • On export_refunds, it allows 1 combination, which no other permit allows.

forbid no_second_refund

An order already refunded is not refunded again

  • On refund_order, it applies to 264 combinations and denies them; a permit applies to 112 of them too.

forbid auditors_do_not_refund

Whoever audits the refunds does not refund

  • On refund_order, it applies to 512 combinations and denies them; a permit applies to 224 of them too.

forbid suspended_staff_do_nothing

A suspended member of the staff does nothing

  • On view_order, it applies to 8 combinations and denies them; a permit applies to 7 of them too.
  • On refund_order, it applies to 512 combinations and denies them; a permit applies to 224 of them too.
  • On export_refunds, it applies to 2 combinations and denies them; a permit applies to 1 of them too.

Computed values

What an action computes and gives Cedar in its context. The generated code computes each one from the service's own data, the operation's arguments and the server's clock, and never takes it from the caller. The pages of the rules and the dates are the pages for people that rulec and koyomi draw, as they draw them.

refund_band of refund_order

  • Written: refund_band = refund_limit(amount: amount, limit: principal.refund_limit).band
  • Computed by rulec "rules/refund_limit.rule" output band, from amount and principal.refund_limit.
  • Its values: within_limit and over_limit; Cedar is given the strings "within_limit" and "over_limit".
  • It is computed only for a principal of type User; for another type, the context does not have it.

in_period of refund_order

  • Written: in_period = today <= refund_terms.last_day(paid_on: resource.paid_on)
  • Computed as today <= koyomi "dates/refund_terms.cal" date last_day: koyomi gives the date from resource.paid_on, and the value is yes when today is on or before it.
  • Today is the date at the offset +00:00 from UTC (the line today).
  • Its values: yes and no (true and false in Cedar).

business_day of refund_order

  • Written: business_day = today is open in uk
  • Computed as today is open in koyomi "calendars/england_and_wales.cal": yes when today is a business day of the calendar.
  • Today is the date at the offset +00:00 from UTC (the line today).
  • Its values: yes and no (true and false in Cedar).

in_period and business_day both read today. Computed by koyomi on every day of the declared ranges, each of the 4 sets of their values can happen.

Expectations, separations and roles

Expectations

  • clerks_never_refund_over_their_limit (expects deny, on refund_order): A clerk who is not a manager never refunds more than the clerk's own limit. It holds on all 128 combinations it picks.
  • nothing_refunded_twice (expects deny, on refund_order): No one refunds an order that is already refunded. It holds on all 264 combinations it picks.
  • managers_refund_in_period (expects allow, on refund_order): A manager who is not suspended and does not audit refunds an order not yet refunded, while the period lasts. It holds on all 48 combinations it picks.

Separations

  • refunding_and_auditing (refund_order, export_refunds): The people who refund are not the people who audit the refunds. It holds: no principal is allowed two of them.

Roles

  • clerk: Answers customers, and refunds small amounts. Its can lists view_order and refund_order.
  • manager: Refunds what a clerk cannot, and after the refund period on a business day. It includes clerk. Its can lists view_order and refund_order.
  • auditor: Checks the refunds after the fact. Its can lists view_order and export_refunds.

Whether a principal holding one role alone (and the roles it includes) is allowed each action: always, in every combination of the rest; sometimes, allowed in some combinations and denied in others; never, denied in every one. - is an action that does not take the principal's type. Where a role writes can, sekisho check holds the actions it is always or sometimes allowed to it.
