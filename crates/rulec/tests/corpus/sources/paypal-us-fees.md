# PayPal merchant fees, United States

Copied from <https://www.paypal.com/us/webapps/mpp/merchant-fees> on 21 September 2026.
The figures are PayPal's own; they are reproduced here as the copy this rule is held to.

## Commercial transaction rates

| What | Rate |
|---|---|
| PayPal Checkout, domestic | 3.49% + fixed fee |
| Fixed fee, USD | 0.49 USD |
| International commercial transaction, in addition to the domestic rate | 1.50% |
