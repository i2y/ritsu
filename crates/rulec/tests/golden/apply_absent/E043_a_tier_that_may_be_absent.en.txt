 The completeness of `tier_credit.rule` was proved where `tier` has a value; its tables have no answer for the absent value.
 `with` reads the absent value as a value of the callee's: `tier = member with none -> <value>`. If it always has a value, declare `member` as tier. A table of this rule that answers `none` too can also decide the value, and pass that.
