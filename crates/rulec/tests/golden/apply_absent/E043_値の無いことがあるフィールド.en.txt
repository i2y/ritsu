 The completeness of `明細のポイント.rule` was proved where the field `明細の段階` of an element has a value; its tables have no answer for the absent value.
 If it always has a value, declare `明細の段階` as 段階. If it can be absent, the callee's field `明細の段階` has to take the absent value and answer it (段階? and a row for `none`), which is a change to the callee.
