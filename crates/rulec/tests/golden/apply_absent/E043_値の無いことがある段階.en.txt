 The completeness of `段階の値引き.rule` was proved where `段階` has a value; its tables have no answer for the absent value.
 `with` reads the absent value as a value of the callee's: `段階 = 会員 with none -> <value>`. If it always has a value, declare `会員` as 段階. A table of this rule that answers `none` too can also decide the value, and pass that.
