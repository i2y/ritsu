 The completeness of `数量の手数料.rule` was proved where `数量` has a value; its tables have no answer for the absent value.
 If it always has a value, declare `個数` as number. If it can be absent, the callee's `数量` has to take the absent value and answer it (number? and a row for `none`), which is a change to the callee.
