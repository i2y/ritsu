 Python: the outputs of a rule with two or more are the fields of `Output`, a `NamedTuple`, and a `NamedTuple` takes no field whose name starts with `_`, so the generated Python file does not load.
 Rust, Swift and Wasm: the outputs of a rule with two or more are the fields of the `Output` struct, and `_` is the pattern that binds nothing: it names no field.
 Go: the outputs of a rule with two or more are the fields of the `Output` struct, and `_` is the blank identifier: a field of that name can be neither written nor read.
 Java: the outputs of a rule with two or more are the components of the `Output` record, and `_` is a keyword.
 Connect: the service runs the Python module, which does not load.
 For a target you do not generate, leave it. For one you do, give it an alias (`_(<alias>)`).
