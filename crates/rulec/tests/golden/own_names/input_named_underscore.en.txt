 Go: the inputs and the sequences are the fields of the `Input` struct, and `_` is the blank identifier: a field of that name can be neither written nor read.
 Swift: the inputs and the sequences are the labels a caller passes the arguments of the rule's function under, and `_` is the label of an argument that has none: it cannot be passed by that name, and the function cannot read it by its name.
 For a target you do not generate, leave it. For one you do, give it an alias (`_(<alias>)`).
