 Rust, Swift and Wasm: `_` is the pattern that binds nothing, and names no variable.
 Go: `_` is the blank identifier, which names no variable and no field.
 Java: `_` is a keyword.
 For a target you do not generate, leave it. For one you do, give it an alias (`_(<alias>)`).
