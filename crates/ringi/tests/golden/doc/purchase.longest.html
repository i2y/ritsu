From 2026-04-03 (Fri) to 2027-02-26 (Fri): 231 business days.

No ticket takes longer than 246 business days, whatever the days the calendar closes; a ticket that takes all of them need not be there. On these dates it takes fewer: where a deadline at the end of a day runs out on the eve of a day the calendar closes, the stage goes up on the closed day, which is not a business day, and so takes one business day less.
