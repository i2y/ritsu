error[E401]: example.ringi:27:3: The claim `ends_in_time` says every ticket ends within 30 business days, and a ticket can take 56
    27 |   ends_in_time : every ticket ends within 30 business days
  = A round takes 14 business days at the most: `check` 7 and `decide` 7.
  = `check` and `decide`: `due in 3 business days at end of day`; late, it goes up on the next day, and is due again counted from there (`when late escalate, then expire`): 3 + 1 + 3 = 7.
  = The ticket goes back 2 times at the most (`send back … at most 2 times`), for 3 rounds, and its requester has 5 business days each time (`resubmit within 5 business days`); it is paused for 4 business days at the most (`pause at most 4 business days`): 3 × 14 + 2 × 5 + 4 = 56.
  = Write 56 business days in the claim, or shorten the deadlines, the times it goes back or the pause.
