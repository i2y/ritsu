Total: it answers every input in the ranges it declares. Its head writes total, and kaname holds it to that.

The amount goes to the seller, and the fee the rule gives for it goes from the seller to the processor.

fn settle(payment: Payment) -> Settlement total
  description "The amount goes to the seller, and the fee the rule gives for it goes from the seller to the processor"
  let fee = card_fee(amount: payment.amount, international: payment.international).fee
  Settlement {
    capture: ledger.capture.do(payment: payment.id, seller: payment.seller, amount: payment.amount),
    fee: ledger.processing_fee.do(payment: payment.id, seller: payment.seller, fee: fee),
  }
