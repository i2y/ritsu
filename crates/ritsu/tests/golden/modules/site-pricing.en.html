Shipping fees and order totals of the Acme shop

Requiring it

ritsu mod add pricing /acme/pricing@1.1.0

It writes this require line in ritsu.mod, and the files name the module's files @pricing/…. To check its signature, write the signature line too.

require pricing oci "/acme/pricing" 1.1.0
signature "/acme/" by matching "^https://github[.]com/acme/" issuer "https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com"
