E107 (error) — A requirement and what meets it read different texts of one article

When: What meets a requirement pins an article the requirement cites, and none of its copies has the text of the requirement's copy: one of them is old.

Fix: Read the diff of the texts, then fetch and pin the older copy again.

Example:
    (The reproduction needs the suite's tools; it is added when yuen reads them.)

See also: E103
