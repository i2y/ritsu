# chobo

`.book` — ledgers: accounts, their bounds, transfers, holds.

**chobo** declares accounts, the bounds each must keep, and the transfers between them, checks that no sequence of transfers, holds and expiries can take an account past a bound, and generates clients for PostgreSQL, TigerBeetle and the MySQL family (MySQL, TiDB and Vitess).

chobo has a site of its own: **[the chobo site](../chobo/)**.
