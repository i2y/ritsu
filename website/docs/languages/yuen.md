# yuen

`.req` — where requirements come from, and what satisfies them.

**yuen** says where each requirement came from, who owns it, and what satisfies and verifies it, with every link pinned by hashes at both ends. It pins the requirements of an OpenSpec spec the same way.

yuen's documents are in the repository: [README](https://github.com/i2y/ritsu/blob/main/crates/yuen/README.md), [the reference](https://github.com/i2y/ritsu/blob/main/crates/yuen/docs/reference.md), [the codes of its diagnostics](https://github.com/i2y/ritsu/blob/main/crates/yuen/docs/codes.md).
