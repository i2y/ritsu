How the approvers are chosen

ApproverWho it is
managerthe requester's manager: the head of the team the requester is mainly in, or of the team above where that head is the requester or there is none
dept_headthe head of the first team of the kind Department (department) above the requester

Each approver is chosen when the stage starts, in the version of the organization in effect then. A proxy in effect that day answers beside them.

Deadlines and reminders

Every stage:

  • It is due 2 business days after it starts, at the end of the day.
  • Its approvers are reminded 1 business day after it starts, at 09:00.
  • When it is late, it goes up to the head above, and its deadline is counted again from then; when it is late again, the ticket expires.
  • When nobody can answer it, it goes up to the head above; at the top of the organization, the stage is skipped.
